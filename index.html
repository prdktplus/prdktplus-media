06

Co-branding with partners

When Prdkt+ appears with a partner logo (sponsorships, hiring meetups, workshops, courses), give both marks equal visual weight, separated by a thin vertical rule.

Partner logo
Side by side. Match heights optically. Keep clear space x between each logo and the rule.
In partnership with
Attribution line. “In partnership with”, “Supported by” or “Community partner” sits above the logo, never inside it.
Share for approvalSend co-branded materials to the Prdkt+ team before publishing.
No implied endorsementDon’t use the logo to suggest Prdkt+ endorses a product or course without agreement.
No merged marksDon’t combine the Prdkt+ logo with another mark into a new logo.
